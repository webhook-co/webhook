Your **display name** and **avatar** are how you appear across the dashboard and to the other members
of your organizations.

## Update them

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your profile">
    Go to your account's **Profile** settings from the account menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit your name">
    Change your display name and save. It updates everywhere you appear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload an avatar">
    Upload a picture to replace the default. It's shown next to your name in the dashboard and on
    your team's members list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Your **email address** is changed separately, because it's tied to how you sign in — see [change
  your email](/help/your-account/change-your-email).
</Note>
