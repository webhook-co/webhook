Pricing is **single-dimension**: you're billed on **events** and nothing else — no per-seat, per-step,
or per-feature charges. For the current plans and prices, see the
[pricing page](https://www.webhook.co/pricing); this page explains how they work.

## How the plans work

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Free" icon="gift">
    A one-time allowance of **5,000 events** to try things properly. It **never resets** — it's a
    real trial, not a perpetual tier. When it's used up, capture pauses rather than billing you.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Pro & Scale" icon="arrow-up-right-dots">
    Self-serve paid plans with a **monthly included volume** of events and longer data retention.
    Upgrade or change plan yourself at any time.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Enterprise" icon="building">
    Committed volume, the longest retention, and custom terms — [contact
    sales](mailto:sales@webhook.co).
  </Card>
  <Card title="Overage" icon="chart-line">
    On paid plans, going past your included volume **pauses capture by default**. You can instead
    turn on **overage** to keep capturing, billed at a flat rate per additional million events. See
    [usage alerts & caps](/help/usage/alerts-and-caps).
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## What's the same on every plan

- **The same product.** Plans differ by included volume and retention, not features.
- **A cap that pauses, not a surprise bill.** You're alerted as you approach your limit; capture
  pauses rather than running up an unexpected charge unless you opt into overage.
- **Retention grows with your plan.** Events are kept for a window that's longer on higher plans (7
  days on Free) — see [data retention](/help/usage/data-retention).

<Note>
  Billing is per **organization** — each has its own plan, usage, and invoices. See
  [organizations](/help/organizations/overview).
</Note>
