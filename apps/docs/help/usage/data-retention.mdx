Captured events — including their stored payloads — are kept for a **retention window**, then
automatically removed. The window **grows with your plan**.

## How long events are kept

- **Free** — events are kept for **7 days**.
- **Paid plans** — a longer window, up to a year on Enterprise. See the
  [pricing page](https://www.webhook.co/pricing) for each plan's exact retention.

After the window passes, older events are pruned automatically. Retention doesn't change your
usage — an event was counted when it was captured, whether or not it's still within the window.

## Deleting an event early

Separately from retention, you can delete an individual event yourself at any time — its content is
redacted immediately and its payload purged shortly after. As with retention, this doesn't lower your
usage (the event was already counted). See [events & usage](/events-and-usage) for the details.

<Note>
  Need a longer window than your plan offers? [Upgrade](/help/billing/manage-your-subscription), or
  forward events to a [destination](/help/getting-started/glossary) you control so you keep your own
  copy.
</Note>
