Create a separate organization when you want an isolated workspace — for a team, a client, or a
project that should keep its endpoints, events, and billing apart from your others.

## Create one

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the organization switcher">
    Use the organization switcher in the dashboard and choose to create a new organization.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it and pick a URL">
    Give it a name and a short **URL slug** (it appears in the address as `/org/your-slug/…`). You
    can [change both later](/help/organizations/rename-and-url).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your team">
    You're the new organization's **owner**. [Invite
    members](/help/organizations/invite-and-manage-members) and assign their
    [roles](/help/organizations/roles-and-permissions).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  On the free plan you can create up to **two** organizations. This counts organizations you create,
  not ones you're invited to.
</Note>
