An organization's **owner** can permanently delete it. This erases the organization and everything it
holds — endpoints, events, stored payloads, destinations, and members' access to it.

<Warning>
  Deleting an organization is permanent and can't be undone. Its events and payloads are erased.
  Export anything you need first.
</Warning>

## Delete it

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open organization settings">
    Go to the organization's **Settings**. You must be an **owner**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the deletion">
    Follow the delete-organization step and confirm. The organization and its data are erased for
    everyone in it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  This is for organizations you created. Your **personal organization** is removed when you [delete
  your account](/help/your-account/delete-your-account), not here. If you're the sole owner of a
  shared organization and want to leave it, make another member an owner first — see [delete your
  account](/help/your-account/delete-your-account) for how.
</Note>
